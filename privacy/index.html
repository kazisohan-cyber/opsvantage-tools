Privacy policy

This policy covers the website tools.opsvantage.net and the tools on it ("the tools"), provided by OpsVantage ("we"). The IOC Triage browser extension has its own privacy policy.

What the tools do with what you paste or upload

The IOC Extractor, Email Header Analyzer, Windows Event Triage, M365 Sign-in Review, Phishing Link Unwrapper, Hunting Query Generator, Payload Decoder and JWT Token Decoder run entirely in your browser. Text you paste and files you open are processed on your device and are not sent to us or anyone else. Closing or reloading the page discards them.

Tools that make network requests

  • SPF, DKIM & DMARC Checker: the domain names you check are looked up directly from your browser through Cloudflare's public DNS resolver (1.1.1.1), falling back to Google Public DNS. Those providers receive the DNS queries under their own privacy policies. We do not.
  • CVE Prioritizer: the CVE IDs you enter are looked up directly from your browser at FIRST (api.first.org, for EPSS scores) and CVE.org (cveawg.mitre.org), and the CISA KEV catalogue is downloaded from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Only CVE IDs are sent, under those services' own privacy policies.
  • M365 Posture Scanner: talks directly to Microsoft. See the section below.
  • Lookup links: the buttons under an indicator (VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB, urlscan and others) open that service in a new tab with the indicator in the address. That service receives it under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you click one.
  • Demo videos are files on this site; they load only when you scroll to them.

What is stored in your browser

We set no cookies. In your browser's local storage the site keeps your light or dark theme choice, the "Prepared by" name for reports if you type one, and a note of which tool pages have already shown you the three quick tips. When you send results from one tool to another, the text is held in this tab's session storage, read once by the next tool and deleted (it also expires after five minutes). During a Microsoft sign-in, a one-time code verifier is held in session storage and deleted as soon as you return.

Hosting, fonts and analytics

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes connection data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks, and may count anonymous, cookie-free page views (Cloudflare Web Analytics). Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts download. Every page has a Content Security Policy that only allows scripts from this site and Cloudflare's analytics; the M365 Posture Scanner page blocks all third-party scripts, analytics included.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, advertising, or tracking across sites.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data.
  • No use of your data to train AI models.

M365 Posture Scanner

The scanner reviews the security configuration of a Microsoft 365 / Microsoft Entra ID tenant when someone with access to that tenant signs in.

How the data flows

  • You sign in with Microsoft. Microsoft issues a short-lived access token directly to your browser tab. No refresh token is requested, and the token is kept in memory only, never written to storage.
  • Your browser calls Microsoft Graph and builds the report on your device. Tenant data never reaches an OpsVantage server; there is no backend that could receive it.
  • For each verified email domain, the scanner runs the same public DNS lookups as the DMARC Checker.
  • Reports exist only on your screen and in files you choose to download. Closing the tab ends the session.

Permissions requested (delegated, read-only)

User.ReadSign you in and read your basic profile
Directory.Read.AllUsers, guests, groups, admin roles and app consents
Policy.Read.AllConditional Access, security defaults, consent and guest settings
AuditLog.Read.AllLast sign-in dates and the MFA registration report
UserAuthenticationMethod.Read.AllWhich MFA methods users have registered
SecurityEvents.Read.AllMicrosoft Secure Score
IdentityRiskyUser.Read.AllUsers flagged at risk by Entra ID Protection

Delegated permissions only work within what the signed-in account can already see. The scanner cannot change, create or delete anything.

Removing access

A tenant administrator can remove the scanner at any time: Microsoft Entra admin center > Enterprise applications > "OpsVantage M365 Posture Scanner" > Properties > Delete. This revokes the consent for the whole tenant.
