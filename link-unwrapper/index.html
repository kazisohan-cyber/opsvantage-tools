What it unwraps and checks

Wrappers

Microsoft Defender Safe Links, Proofpoint URL Defense v1, v2 and v3 (including its encoded characters), Barracuda, Symantec, Google, Facebook, LinkedIn and Slack redirects, Base64-encoded URLs, and any parameter that is itself a full URL. Mimecast, Cisco and shorteners keep the target on their servers, so it says so instead of guessing.

Destination checks

Lookalikes of well-known brands, punycode hosts and how they really display, user@host tricks, bare IP addresses, unusual ports, plain HTTP, deep subdomain chains, links straight to HTML or ISO files, and free hosting often used for phishing pages.

Safe by design

No request is made to any link, so nothing on the other end learns you looked. When you need to see the page itself, the urlscan button opens a sandboxed scan in a new tab.
