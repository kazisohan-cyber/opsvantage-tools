Payload Decoder

Paste an encoded PowerShell command, an obfuscated script line or a blob from an alert. Base64, -EncodedCommand (UTF-16LE), gzip and deflate, hex, URL encoding, HTML entities, character codes and reversed strings are recognised and peeled off one layer at a time, then the result is scanned for indicators and risky behaviour.

● Runs in your browser. Nothing is executed and nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

What it decodes

Automatic layers

Each pass picks the most likely encoding: a PowerShell -EncodedCommand argument, a FromBase64String() call, a whole Base64 or hex string, URL encoding, HTML entities, char-code arrays or reversed text. It keeps going until nothing more can be decoded, up to ten layers.

Compression and text

Decoded bytes are checked for gzip, zlib and raw deflate, the way PowerShell loaders usually pack payloads, and for UTF-16LE, which -EncodedCommand always uses. Executables and archives are named, never shown or run.

What it does

The final text is scanned for download cradles, Invoke-Expression, hidden windows and policy bypass, AMSI tampering, Mimikatz, abused Windows tools, Defender changes, backup deletion and persistence, plus every IP, domain, URL and hash in it.
