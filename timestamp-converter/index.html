Formats it understands

Unix and Windows

Unix epoch in seconds, milliseconds, microseconds and nanoseconds (Linux logs, CloudTrail, Splunk, Sentinel exports), Unix hex, and Windows FILETIME: 100-nanosecond intervals since 1601, used by Active Directory attributes such as lastLogonTimestamp, pwdLastSet and accountExpires, by Event Log XML and by the registry, in decimal or hex.

Browsers and Apple

Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers store history and cookie times as microseconds since 1601 (WebKit time). Safari, iOS and macOS use Mac absolute time, seconds since 2001. Excel and OLE dates are days since 1899.

Detection you can check

The format is picked from the size of the number. When a value could mean more than one thing, every plausible reading is listed with its date, so you choose the one that fits the evidence. Special Active Directory values such as 0 and 9223372036854775807 (never) are named.
