Paste the raw headers of a suspicious email, or drop the .eml file. How to read the result

DMARC is the one that matters

DMARC pass means the visible From domain really sent it. DMARC fail on a known brand is the strongest single spoofing signal.

Reply-To mismatch

A Reply-To pointing at a different domain from the From address is the classic business email compromise setup: replies go to the attacker.

Originating IP

The first public IP in the path is usually the sender. Check it with IOC Triage or your threat-intel tools before blocking.
