How these tools treat your data

Security people paste sensitive things. These tools are built so you never have to wonder where that went.

Processed in your browser

Pasted text and uploaded logs are analysed on your machine. They are not sent to or stored on an OpsVantage server.

Only lookups leave

Tools that need the internet, such as a DNS check, send just that query, and each tool says so on the page.

No accounts, no cookies, no ads

No sign-up, no ad scripts, no cross-site tracking. Our host counts anonymous page views; the privacy policy has the details.
