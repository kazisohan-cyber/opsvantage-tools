IOC Extractor

Paste a threat report, alert, email or log. Every IP, domain, URL, hash, email address, CVE, file name, registry key, ASN and Bitcoin address comes out grouped and de-duplicated, defanged or refanged, ready for your ticket or blocklist, with one-click lookups in VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB and more.

● Runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere; lookup links open only when you click one.

What it handles

Defanged input

Understands hxxp, [.], (.), {.}, [dot], (dot), [@], (at) and [:] so you can paste straight from threat intel reports.

Fewer false positives

Checks domains against the full IANA TLD list and skips log field names like event.type, version numbers and clock times. File names such as invoice.pdf get their own list instead of posing as domains.

Safe sharing

"Copy text with IOCs defanged" returns your original text with every indicator neutralised, so links can't be clicked by accident in tickets or chat.
