What gets checked

SPF

One valid record, a strict ending (-all), and fewer than 10 DNS lookups so receivers don't reject it with a permerror.

DMARC

The policy that tells receivers to quarantine or reject spoofed mail, whether it covers 100% of mail, and whether you get reports.

DKIM & extras

Signing keys on 20+ common selectors (Microsoft 365, Google, SendGrid, Mailchimp and more) and their strength, plus MTA-STS, TLS-RPT and BIMI.
