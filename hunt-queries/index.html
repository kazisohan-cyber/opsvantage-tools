Hunting Query Generator

Paste a list of indicators, or a whole threat report. You get hunting queries for Microsoft Sentinel and Defender XDR (KQL), Splunk (SPL), CrowdStrike Falcon LogScale and Elastic, split by indicator type, with the time window you choose.

● Runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

What it generates

Microsoft (KQL)

Advanced hunting queries over DeviceNetworkEvents, DeviceEvents DNS, DeviceFileEvents, DeviceProcessEvents, EmailEvents, EmailUrlInfo, UrlClickEvents, CommonSecurityLog, SigninLogs and DnsEvents. Tables your workspace does not have are skipped automatically.

Splunk, CrowdStrike, Elastic

SPL using Splunk CIM field names plus a faster tstats search over the Network_Traffic data model, Falcon LogScale searches over NetworkConnect, DnsRequest and ProcessRollup2 events, and Kibana KQL on ECS fields.

Clean input

Indicators are pulled out of any text, refanged and de-duplicated. Domains inside URLs are added to the domain search, and private IPs are left out unless you ask for them.

Verdicts before you hunt

IOC Triage, our free browser extension, checks each indicator against 15 threat-intel sources from a right-click, so you hunt only for the bad ones.
