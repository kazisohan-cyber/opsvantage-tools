Put the DMARC checker on your site

MSPs and IT teams can add our SPF, DKIM and DMARC checker to their own website with one snippet. Visitors check their domain without leaving your page, and the results link to the full report with the exact DNS records to fix.

Preview

Copy the snippet

Paste this where the checker should appear. It works on WordPress (Custom HTML block), Webflow, Squarespace, Wix and plain HTML.

Privacy and terms

  
The widget runs in your visitor's browser. Domain names go straight to public DNS resolvers (Cloudflare 1.1.1.1, with Google Public DNS as a fallback), exactly as on our DMARC checker. It sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, so it doesn't add anything to your cookie banner. Please keep the credit line under the frame. Free to use on any site; see the terms.
